## More organized activities

<box gap={3}>
  <box gap={1}>
    **GW Biking: Rock Creek Ride**

    <text color="secondary" size="xs">Saturday, 11 AM to 1 PM, Foggy Bottom</text>

    A 10-mile round-trip ride through Rock Creek, including uphill sections. Moderate difficulty, approximately two hours. Capital Bikeshare is an option. The event is hosted through George Washington University, so confirm whether community members can join. <Cite ref="turn844964search0"/>

## My suggested weekend

**Saturday:** Start with November in Nature at 9 AM for an easy social hike, or choose the 2 PM ranger-led bicycle ride if you'd rather cycle. Both are free.

**Sunday:** The After Emancipation History Hike is the most distinctive outing. If you'd rather get a tennis session in, choose Capital Tennis Association's Sunday doubles.

For a change of scenery, Huntley Meadows would be my first self-guided choice this week. It offers a completely different wetland environment from the wooded trails featured in last week's report.
