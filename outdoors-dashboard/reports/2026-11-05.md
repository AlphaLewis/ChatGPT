## My suggested weekend combination

**Saturday:** WABA's 10-mile Cider Ride if you want a lively group event, or the Outside Hours hike if you prefer a more conversational morning.

**Sunday:** Explore Patuxent North Tract for a quiet forest outing, or play Sunday singles at Rock Creek if tennis is the priority.

My strongest suggestion is **WABA Cider Ride on Saturday and Patuxent North Tract on Sunday**. That gives you one social activity and one peaceful nature experience, with two distinctly different environments.
