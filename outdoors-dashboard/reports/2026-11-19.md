# DMV Weekend Outdoors

**Research date:** Thursday, November 19, 2026, America/New_York
**Weekend:** Saturday, November 21 and Sunday, November 22, 2026

## Top recommendations

1. **Woodlawn Underground Railroad guided hike**, Saturday 10 to 11:30 AM, 2 miles, $8 to $10. This is a strong history and hiking combination on natural-surface trails, about 20 to 25 miles and 35 to 50 minutes from central DC. Difficulty easy to moderate. Official event: https://montgomeryparks.org/events/guided-hike-on-the-underground-railroad-experience-trail-november-21/
2. **Forest Bathing at Locust Grove**, Saturday 10 AM to noon, $10. Guided mindful nature experience on moderate, uneven dirt and rock trails, including some stairs, about 12 to 15 miles and 25 to 40 minutes from central DC. Registration required, ages 18+. Official event: https://montgomeryparks.org/events/forest-bathing-fall-forest-bathing-3/
3. **Bike with a Ranger: Fall Leaves**, Sunday 2 PM, 3.6 miles, free, one hour, easy. Peirce Barn in Rock Creek Park, about 6 miles and 20 to 30 minutes from central DC. Bring a bike and helmet or arrange a rental. This is a recurring NPS program, not a newly launched event. Official calendar: https://home.nps.gov/rocr/planyourvisit/special-events-2026.htm

## Saturday, November 21

- **Roots to Rocks group mountain bike ride**, 1 to 3 PM at Black Hill Regional Park, Boyds. Free coached mountain biking with some bikes and helmets to borrow. Activity mileage not listed. This is a youth-oriented program (ages 6+) and adults should verify participation rules before booking. Approx. 30 to 40 miles, 45 to 65 minutes from central DC. Difficulty depends on skill and trail conditions. https://montgomeryparks.org/events/roots-to-rocks-youth-mountain-bike-ride-roots-to-rocks-group-mountain-bike-ride-13/
- **Capital Hiking Club Compton Peak hike**, 8 AM to 6 PM organized day trip to Shenandoah National Park, Virginia. Strenuous day, trail mileage and registration price require confirmation with organizer. Approx. 80 to 90 miles, 80 to 95 minutes driving to trail area, potentially outside 90 minutes with traffic. Distinctive columnar basalt geology. https://www.capitalhikingclub.org/hikes-schedule
- **Kingman Island cleanup with WABA Trail Rangers**, 10 AM to noon, free and easy volunteer work along Anacostia Riverwalk and Kingman/Heritage Islands. Approx. 4 miles, 15 to 25 minutes from central DC. Worth considering for a social community activity. https://capitalnature.org/cal/week/2026-11-19/
- **Woodlawn guided hike** and **Forest Bathing** both start at 10 AM. Choose one rather than planning both.

## Self-guided outings

- **US National Arboretum walking circuit**, Saturday or Sunday in published visitor hours, choose roughly 2 to 4 miles on gardens and connecting paths, easy, 1 to 2 hours, free. About 5 miles and 15 to 25 minutes from central DC. A flexible low-cost backup if group events fill. Confirm entrances and seasonal hours. https://www.usna.usda.gov/visit/
- **Anacostia Riverwalk Trail**, Saturday or Sunday, choose 8 to 16 miles on paved trails, easy, about 1 to 2 hours riding, free. Multiple DC access points 10 to 25 minutes away. Great for a self-guided ride with river views. Check trail closures. https://www.anacostiawaterfront.org/
