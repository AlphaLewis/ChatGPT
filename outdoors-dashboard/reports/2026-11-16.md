# DMV Weekend Outdoors
Research date: Monday, November 16, 2026 (America/New_York)
Weekend covered: Saturday, November 21, and Sunday, November 22, 2026
Run type: Manual end-to-end dashboard publication test. The original Thursday scheduled research remains unchanged.
Scope: Hiking, cycling, nature and tennis around Washington, DC; up to about 90 minutes driving.

## Executive overview
A varied shortlist for November 21 and 22. Standouts: the $8 to $10 Underground Railroad guided hike at Woodlawn Manor, the $10 forest-bathing program at Locust Grove, the social E-bike Lovers ride to Wolf Trap, and Sunday's Little Paint Branch group ride. A free tennis social takes place on Friday evening as an optional early-weekend bonus.

All distance and driving estimates are approximate from central Washington, DC, not live traffic. Event slots, weather cancellations, skill requirements and fees should be rechecked before departure.

## Top three picks
1. **Woodlawn Manor guided Underground Railroad hike**, Saturday Nov 21, 10 to 11:30 AM, 2 miles, natural surface, easy to moderate, $8 to $10. About 21 miles and 35 to 50 minutes from DC. Learn navigation and survival techniques used by freedom seekers, museum included. [Official event](https://montgomeryparks.org/events/guided-hike-on-the-underground-railroad-experience-trail-november-21/).
2. **Forest Bathing at Locust Grove Nature Center**, Saturday Nov 21, 10 AM to noon, slow mindful walk, moderate uneven terrain with occasional stairs, route length not stated, $10. About 12 miles and 25 to 40 minutes. For grounding and contemplative outdoor time. [Official Montgomery Parks listing](https://montgomeryparks.org/events/forest-bathing-fall-forest-bathing-3/).
3. **E-bike Lovers ride to Wolf Trap**, Saturday Nov 21, 10 AM to 1 PM, 28 miles, moderate, bring bike, meet Lindway Terrace Park in McLean. About 12 miles and 25 to 40 minutes from central DC. Group cycling with a picnic stop, good for riders comfortable with distance. Cost not stated. [Organizer listing](https://www.meetup.com/electric-bicycle-e-bike-lovers-washington-dc/events/317363591/).

## Saturday, November 21
- **Fabulosity Bike Ride**, 10 AM to 3 PM, starts National Museum of African American History and Culture, Washington, DC, about 2 miles and 10 to 20 minutes from central DC. Social group ride, difficulty and route mileage not published, allow up to 5 hours. Cost not published. Organizer registration window Nov 20 at 10 AM to Nov 21 at 9 AM. [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/fabulosity-bike-rides/events/316612021/).
- **Bull Run Occoquan Trail, self-guided**, select a 2 to 4-mile natural-surface wooded segment, 1 to 2 hours, easy to moderate, Fairfax County approach about 25 to 35 miles and 40 to 60 minutes driving. No scheduled event or reservation needed; confirm trail and parking rules before departure. [Official NOVA Parks trail page](https://www.novaparks.com/parks/bull-run-occoquan-trail).
- **Woodlawn Manor and Forest Bathing** are competing 10 AM programs. Do not schedule both. Details and links above.

## Sunday, November 22
- **Little Paint Branch Trail cycling meetup**, 11 AM to 2 PM, starts Fairland Recreational Park, Burtonsville, about 13 to 17 miles and 25 to 40 minutes from DC. Choose 12, 16, 20 or 32 miles round trip. Moderate, hilly northern segments are not beginner-friendly. Allow 2 to 4 hours. Free parking, group-event fee not listed. Organizers may postpone for rainfall above 49% or winds above 15 mph. [Meetup with route and weather instructions](https://www.meetup.com/md-rail-trail-biking-club/events/313764204/).
- **Great Falls Park volunteer-guided bird walk**, 8 AM, approximately 3 hours, suitable for beginners, walk itself free but NPS entrance fee applies. About 18 to 22 miles and 30 to 50 minutes. Route mileage not specified; bring comfortable shoes and binoculars if available. [Official NPS event](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=25675C61-09EB-8A68-D0D7DE78C187294F).
- **Travels with Ollie at the National Arboretum**, 10:30 AM to noon, free, easy guided group stroll, mileage not published, R Street Gate, approximately 5 miles and 15 to 25 minutes from central DC. Dog-friendly but no dog required stated; check organizer details. [Eventbrite listing](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/travelswithollie-november-group-hike-registration-2002456187361).
- **Bike with a Ranger: Fall Leaves**, 2 PM, 3.6-mile round trip, easy, one hour, free, Peirce Barn (2401 Tilden Street NW), approximately 6 miles and 20 to 30 minutes. Bring helmet and working bike or rent Capital Bikeshare. This recurring event also appeared in earlier research for a different date, and should not be reported as a newly created program. [Official NPS event](https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/event-details.htm?id=23C4388C-A13B-514F-9C808DCD78598F96).
